# AI literacy decks

*Part of the [AI Enablement Toolkit](../../README.md).*

Two editions of the same 19-slide deck, for teams and decision makers without a technical background: how AI systems work, where they fail, and how to use them responsibly. They share their structure, design and glossary, and differ in language and context.

## Structure

The order puts governance and risk before tools, and prompting after both, so people learn what can go wrong before they learn how to go faster.

## What to know before using them

- **The Spanish deck is written for a Costa Rican audience.** The national strategy and local sources set its context. The English deck replaces them with international sources, so use it for any other country.
- **Their references were checked in October 2026.** The title, publisher and year of each one were confirmed on the publisher's own page, and statistics that could not be confirmed there were removed. The OECD quotations in the English deck were checked word for word against the OECD's pages.
- **Each deck is a single self-contained HTML file.** Open it in a browser; no install, no network needed once downloaded.
